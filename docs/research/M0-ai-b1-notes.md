## Likely strengths

- “A short email” for a “client meeting” gives E01 a plausible workplace context for a B1 adult.
- “Do not use the previous version” and “The attachment name ended in v3” give a concrete distinction a learner can carry into later evidence.
- “manh mối đầu tiên là email trên bàn có vòng sáng” and the `HỒ SƠ · EMAIL` tutorial label point to a clear first action.
- The “8–12 PHÚT” estimate communicates a bounded first session, though actual duration remains untested.

## Unknowns

- Actual B1 comprehension, deductions, interest, frustration, enjoyment, and completion are unknown until human testing.
- The packet cannot establish movement feel, collision, action pacing, whether dodge interrupts thought, checkpoint feedback, character acting, sound quality, or how readable clues are in motion.
- No later evidence, suspect resolution, score rule, translation control in the current baseline, retention, or willingness to pay is available from this packet.
- These hypotheses should guide questions for 5–8 consenting target learners in M1; they are not a substitute for those sessions.
