# M0 AI learner audit protocol

Status: active simulated review, 2026-09-27. The owner approved shortening M0 and using AI agents as learner stand-ins. This is a heuristic review, not a usability study or evidence of enjoyment, retention, or willingness to pay.

## Inputs and roles

- Give each role only [the public first-session packet](M0-player-packet.md), never the case solution, private answers, full case JSON, other role notes, or source code.
- **A2 learner:** adult preparing for work; attends to the first goal, short office English, version terms, and where translation would help.
- **B1 learner:** working adult; attends to whether the clue supports a deduction, whether the mystery creates a reason to continue, and whether action interrupts thought.
- Optional entertainment-first skeptic: tests whether character acting, sound, pacing, and feedback make this feel like a game. Keep this role separate from learner evidence.
- Roles write independent reports. The integration owner compares their claims with the visible product and labels contradictions.

## Report format

For each finding record: exact screen or quoted clue anchor; likely interpretation; possible effect; confidence/uncertainty; a concrete M1 human-test question. Mark anything beyond the supplied packet as **unknown**. Do not invent a completed playthrough, actual emotion, audio heard, frame rate, price intent, or a percentage of players.

## Evidence boundary

Visible browser observations, source/content inspection, and AI role hypotheses are separate evidence types. Agreement between AI roles is a prioritization signal only. Any M0 recommendation should be reversible until 5–8 target learners test the M1 segment. M1 sessions need consent, deidentified notes, no recording by default, and a separately approved recruitment/compensation route. No participant contact or spending is authorized here.
