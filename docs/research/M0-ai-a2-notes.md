## Likely strengths

- The first action is explicitly named in “Di chuyển tới nhãn HỒ SƠ · EMAIL…” and supported by the described EMAIL marker. This may reduce navigation ambiguity for a first-time player.
- E01 is short and uses workplace nouns: “email,” “team,” “report,” “client meeting,” and “attachment.” That gives an A2 learner a concrete situation to discuss.
- The glossary supplies Vietnamese meanings for “approved,” “previous,” “attachment,” and “version,” the terms most likely to affect understanding of E01.
- “Do not use the previous version” and “ended in v3” give two textual anchors for checking which version the team should use.

## Unknowns

- Actual A2 comprehension, reading time, help-seeking, and ability to reach or collect E01 are unknown.
- The packet cannot establish the game's in-motion input, collision, interaction feedback, checkpoint clarity, acting, sound quality, or performance.
- Enjoyment, desire to continue, retention, and willingness to pay are unknown; this simulated review must not be counted as participant evidence.
- The proposed Vietnamese reveal is not in the baseline, so its usability and effect remain unknown.
